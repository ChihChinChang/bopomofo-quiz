Before You Begin

Learner's Guide

🧠 Your Brain Is Like a Rubber Band — Highly Plastic

  1. Your brain can always stretch further. Neuroplasticity means every repetition builds new neural pathways. No matter your age, your brain can learn any language. The only enemy is giving up. Make mistakes boldly — they are the mechanism of progress.
  2. Imitate, repeat, and embrace errors. Watch native speakers' mouths carefully. Mimic sounds before you understand them. Errors are not failure — they are proof your brain is stretching.
  3. Build up from memory. Once pronunciation is solid, recall without notes. True fluency lives in your head, not on the page.
  4. Listen first, speak second. Mouth along while listening — fluency beats accuracy at this stage. Fill your life with Chinese: podcasts, films, native-speaker conversations. Even passive listening builds instinct.
  5. Study in focused blocks. Minimum one hour per session. Stop when you feel genuinely tired or bored. No need to push past three hours.

Take-Home Assignment

回家任務 Homework

📚 Three Tasks Before Next Class

  1. Memorise all Bopomofo symbols ㄅㄆㄇㄈ. Cover the page and write every symbol from memory. Be able to recite the full alphabet in under two minutes without looking.
  2. Memorise all vocabulary — write and read aloud. For each word: (1) see the English, write the Chinese character from memory, (2) write the Bopomofo, (3) say it aloud with the correct tone. Do not move on until all three steps are correct without peeking.
  3. Memorise all sentences and vocabulary from this lesson. Cover the Bopomofo and recite every poem line and every dialogue sentence from memory. Aim for fluency — meaning flows without looking at the page.
